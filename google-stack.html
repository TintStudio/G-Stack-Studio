├── 📁 [Brand] - [Keyword 2] Hub
├── 📁 [Brand] - [Keyword 3] Hub
└── 🔗 Circular Links Cross-Silo
Best for: High-competition multi-city franchises and national brands.
Crawl Loop Mechanics

Circular Silo Interlinking Engine

When manual stacks are built, links are often haphazardly dumped or broken. G-Stack Studio executes a mathematically structured circular silo loop during build time:

1
Sequential Silo Chaining: Keyword 1 Doc links forward to Keyword 2 Doc, Keyword 2 links to Keyword 3, and the final Keyword links back to Keyword 1.
2
Hub Anchor Integration: Every keyword article links back to the central Master Google Sheet Directory and the Root Drive folder.
3
Entity Citation Grounding: Every single asset contains verified NAP (Name, Address, Phone) citations and links to client Google Business Profiles.
Honest Engineering Notice: G-Stack Studio automates the technical formatting, public deployment, and circular interlinking of standard Google Drive files. We do not promise magical search rankings or instant Map Pack indexing. Results depend on local keyword competition, content quality, and organic domain signals.
