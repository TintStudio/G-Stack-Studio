How Monthly Subscriptions Work:

Quotas & Limits: Google Stacks and Cloud Sites quotas reset automatically every 30 days. Unused quotas do not roll over.

Pages Per Site: The maximum number of interconnected HTML pages the cloud engine will compile and publish within a single website project.

Hardware Locks: Each subscription is paired with 1 PC device lock. You receive 1 free machine transfer every 30 days via the automated self-service system.

Cancellations: You can manage or cancel your recurring subscription anytime in 1-click via the Gumroad customer billing portal.
