Google API Compliance Verified

Privacy Policy & Google API User Data Disclosures

Last Updated: September 2026 | Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Google API Services User Data Policy.

Core Google User Data Commitment

G-Stack Studio does not store, sell, share, or transmit user Google account credentials, authentication tokens, project data, or personal files to external cloud databases or third-party servers. All Google OAuth tokens (token.json) are stored strictly in local application storage on the user's local operating system.

1. Introduction & Scope

G-Stack Studio ("we", "our", or "the Software") is a professional desktop automation application created to help search engine optimization (SEO) agencies, marketing consultants, and business owners automate the deployment of Google Cloud asset silos and entity stacks. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict policies regarding the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of data when you utilize our desktop application and website (https://gstackstudio.com).

2. Google API Services User Data Policy & Scopes

G-Stack Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

A. Specific Scopes Requested & Functional Purpose:

The desktop software requests only the specific scopes required to automate authority stacks on your behalf:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive

Purpose: Allows G-Stack Studio to create root authority folders, establish 13-subfolder taxonomy silos, upload client documents/spreadsheets, and adjust folder sharing settings to public viewing (required for search engine indexation).

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents

Purpose: Used exclusively to generate, format, and insert geo-optimized authority articles, H1/H2 header hierarchies, and circular silo cross-links into Google Docs within your Drive.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets

Purpose: Generates the Master Google Sheets Directory containing the full catalog of asset links, anchor text variations, and citation mapping nodes.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar

Purpose: Automatically publishes public business operating hours, seasonal specials, and localized event signals to Google Calendar to reinforce local entity trust.

B. Local OAuth Token Storage & Zero Cloud Exposure:

When you authenticate your Google Account via our 1-click OAuth flow, the authorization token (token.json) is written exclusively to your local hard drive (within your local operating system user directory, e.g., AppData\Roaming\GStackStudio on Windows or ~/Library/Application Support/GStackStudio on macOS).

  • Google OAuth tokens are NEVER sent to or stored on G-Stack Studio web servers.
  • We do not have access to your Google account password.
  • We do not monitor, read, or catalog your private personal Drive files outside of the specific campaign folder created by the software.
  • You can instantly revoke G-Stack Studio's access at any time via your Google Account Security Dashboard.

3. Information Collected on Our Website

When purchasing a software license or submitting an inquiry via our contact form, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Your name, agency name, and email address for software license key delivery.
  • Payment Information: Financial transactions are processed securely via PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors (such as Stripe or Paddle). G-Stack Studio never sees, stores, or processes raw credit card details.
  • Web Analytics: Aggregated, anonymized website visit statistics (IP address, browser type, referring pages) via privacy-centric analytics to enhance our site performance.

4. Third-Party AI Engine Keys (OpenAI & Gemini)

If you opt to utilize the built-in AI content writer, your custom OpenAI or Google Gemini API keys are encrypted using native OS-level keychain cryptography and stored 100% locally on your machine. API calls to generate articles are transmitted directly from your client machine to OpenAI or Google's endpoints over HTTPS.

5. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under applicable international privacy laws, you possess the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold regarding your software license.
  • Request immediate deletion or rectification of your customer account profile.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.
  • Because all campaign assets and Google tokens reside on your local machine, you have complete autonomous control to delete all local folders and files at will.
