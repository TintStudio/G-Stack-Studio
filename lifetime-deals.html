Frequently Asked Questions About Lifetime Deals

No asterisks, hidden recurring maintenance charges, or deceptive fine print.

What does "Lifetime" mean for G-Stack Studio?

A lifetime license gives you perpetual commercial rights to run the G-Stack Studio Windows desktop application without ever paying a recurring monthly or annual subscription fee. You receive perpetual access to all future v2.x and v3.x feature updates.

Why do monthly quotas still apply to a lifetime license?

Every lifetime plan includes a set monthly quota (for example, 50 Google Stacks and 100 Cloud Sites per month on Agency Lifetime). These quotas reset automatically every 30 days. Monthly limits protect third-party API stability (Google Workspace, AWS, Cloudflare) and prevent bot spam abuse while ensuring sustainable lifetime service.

How many computers can I activate my lifetime license on?

Starter, Pro, and Ultimate Lifetime licenses include 1 PC activation locked to your machine's hardware ID. Agency Lifetime includes 2 PC device activations. The Ultimate Agency Fleet tier includes 5 separate commercial license keys for 5 separate workstations.

What happens if I upgrade or change my computer?

Every license includes 1 free machine transfer every 30 days. If you buy a new laptop or format Windows, simply launch G-Stack Studio on your new PC, enter your registered email and key, and click Transfer Machine.

Does Starter Lifetime include Cloud Stacking or Cloud Sites?

No. Starter Lifetime includes all 20 Google Drive asset types, AI writing tools, and white-label reports, but does not include Cloud Sites, Cloud Stacking, or the Batch Builder. If you need static multi-cloud website generation, choose Pro Lifetime ($449) or higher.
