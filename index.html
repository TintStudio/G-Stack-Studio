How G-Stack Studio Works

An intuitive 3-phase automated desktop pipeline built for speed and privacy.

01

Enter Project & NAP Details

Input the business name, address, phone, GBP Maps URL, and primary keyword targets. Save to your local client database for 1-click re-runs.

Connect Required Accounts

Frequently Asked Questions

What operating systems are supported?

G-Stack Studio is built natively for 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11. It operates as a portable desktop application with zero heavy database installations required.

What is the difference between Monthly and Lifetime licenses?

Monthly plans are recurring subscriptions that can be cancelled anytime. Lifetime deals are one-time payments providing perpetual software access. On both plan types, your stack and cloud site limits reset every 30 days.

Does the Starter plan include Cloud Stacking?

No. Starter (both Monthly $49 and Lifetime $299) is strictly dedicated to Google Entity Stacks (all 20 asset types). If you require multi-cloud static website generation across AWS, Netlify, and Cloudflare, choose Pro ($99/mo or $449 Lifetime) or higher.
