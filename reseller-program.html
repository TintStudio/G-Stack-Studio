Reseller Program FAQ

How do I generate keys for my customers?

Once approved, you log in to the Reseller Portal via secure partner credentials using your Reseller Master Key. Choose the plan tier (Starter, Pro, Agency, Ultimate), enter the customer's email, and click Create License. You can also paste up to 100 customer emails for instant bulk generation and CSV download.

Who provides customer support for end users?

Resellers handle first-line customer communication, billing, and onboarding for their own clients. Our engineering team provides dedicated backend support to the reseller for software updates, server syncs, and license reset escalations.

Can I sell in any country or territory?

Yes. G-Stack Studio is used by SEO professionals across North America, Europe, Asia, Australia, and worldwide. There are no geographic territory restrictions.
