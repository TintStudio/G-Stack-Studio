Installation to Client Delivery Walkthrough

Follow our verified 8-step pipeline: download the desktop application, activate your license key, connect your local Google OAuth credentials, and output complete multi-page stacks in under 3 minutes.

01
Step 1 · Desktop Setup

Install the Windows Desktop Application

Download the lightweight zip file provided upon purchase. Extract to any directory on your Windows 10/11 workstation (e.g. C:\GStackStudio\). No complicated runtime dependencies, database configurations, or background server setups required.

Compatibility: Windows 10 / Windows 11 (64-bit) · Portable Execution
02
Step 2 · Machine Lock

License Activation & Device Pairing

Enter your Registered Email Address and Gumroad Transaction ID/License Key in the ScrapeBox-style activation GUI. The software computes a secure Hardware Identifier (HWID) based on your CPU and Motherboard UUID, binding your license for 0.01-second instant offline launching.

Machine Policy: Standard licenses include 1 PC lock. You receive 1 free machine transfer every 30 days if you switch computers. Agency Lifetime includes 2 activations; Ultimate Agency includes 5 separate licenses.
03
Step 3 · Google Authorization

Connect Your Google Cloud OAuth Project

In Google Cloud Console, enable the 6 Workspace APIs (Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Calendar). Download your desktop client credentials.json. Launch G-Stack Studio, click Authorize, and complete the standard Google consent prompt in your default browser.

Privacy Guarantee: Authorization tokens are stored locally in token.json on your PC. No third-party cloud server ever intercepts your Google credentials or client data.
04
Step 4 · Intake Configuration

Configure Agency & Client Intake Profiles

Add your client's business information: Name, Address, Phone (NAP), website URL, Google Business Profile Maps URL, primary keyword targets, LSI terms, and existing social citation links. Saved profiles can be edited, cloned, and re-run whenever new campaigns launch.

05
Step 5 · Architecture Selection

Select Desired Assets & Silo Architecture

Tick the exact Google assets you wish to assemble (Select All for the full 20-asset ecosystem). Choose your architecture mode: Single Authority Hub, Keyword Stacks Inside Main Folder, or Keyword Stacks in Separate Folders. Connect your Gemini or OpenAI API key if generating unique articles.

06
Step 6 · Automated Execution

Run Automation Engine with Live Progress

Click Build Stack. The desktop engine communicates with Google APIs: creating folders, writing Docs, formatting Sheets, building Slides, generating vector Drawings, publishing to the web, setting public reader permissions, and executing circular reciprocal interlinking.

07
Step 7 · Deliverable Export

Export 9-Tab Excel & White-Label PDF Reports

Within 120 seconds, the software compiles a master 9-tab branded Excel directory and an executive client PDF report. Every published URL, QR code, and iframe embed snippet is organized for instantaneous client handover.

08
Step 8 · Cloud Publishing (Eligible Plans)

Deploy Multi-Page Static Cloud Authority Sites

On Pro, Agency, or Ultimate plans: tick your target cloud hosting providers (AWS S3, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, Backblaze B2, Firebase, or Neocities). Click Deploy to publish lightning-fast static HTML sites interlinked directly with your newly created Google Stack.

* Note: Cloud Publishing is not included in the entry-level Starter plan.
